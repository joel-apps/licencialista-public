Common questions

Which exams are covered?

The national written cosmetology theory exam used by California, Illinois and other states, and the Texas written cosmetology exam, including Texas laws and rules. You choose your exam when you first open the app, and can change it in Settings. Practical (hands-on) exams are not covered. Check with your state board which exam you will take.

Are these the real exam questions?

No. Every question is an original practice question written for this app. Each answer cites the public source it is based on, such as a state law or a published exam content outline.

How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?

Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple ID you bought with, open Settings in the app, tap Unlock cosmetology, then Restore purchase.

How do I report an error in a question?

Tap Report an error under the question. Your mail app opens with the question’s code in the subject; tell us what looks wrong and send it. We check every report against the cited source and fix confirmed errors in an update. You can also email us directly.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. All questions, explanations, the glossary and read-aloud work offline. Only buying or restoring the unlock needs a connection.

Is it a subscription?

No. At least 10 questions in every topic and one 25-question mini mock exam are free. A single purchase unlocks everything for good.

Can I get a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple, so refunds are too. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Does “¿Listo?” mean I will pass?

No. It is an estimate based on your recent practice answers, to help you decide what to study. It cannot predict or guarantee your exam result.
