Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This policy explains what the Licencia Lista iPhone app does with your data. Licencia Lista is published by Joel Hernandez (“we”), an individual developer. Contact: joelhcode@gmail.com.

In short: your answers and progress stay on your iPhone. No account, no ads, no tracking across other apps or websites, and we never sell your data. The App Store version sends a few anonymous usage statistics that never include your answers.

Your answers and progress

Your answers, scores, review schedule, readiness estimate and settings are stored on your iPhone. If you are signed in to iCloud, a copy of your answers and settings is also kept in your own iCloud account (Apple’s iCloud key-value storage), so your progress follows your Apple ID to your other iPhones and comes back if you reinstall the app. That copy is stored by Apple under your Apple ID; we cannot see or access it. None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. The readiness estimate is calculated on your iPhone.

Read-aloud

Read-aloud uses your iPhone’s built-in voice. The text is spoken on the device and is not sent anywhere.

Reporting an error

Report an error opens your own mail app with a message to us. Nothing is sent unless you send that email. If you do, we receive your email address and what you write, and use them only to reply and to fix the question.

Anonymous usage statistics

We use PostHog to understand how the app is used. Only the App Store version of the app sends statistics; test versions send none. It sends only these events: when you finish the welcome screen, with the exam you chose (national or Texas), the exam language, the app language and a rough exam-date range (not sure, about 2 weeks, 1 month, 3 months or later); when you start topic practice and which topic; when you start a review of missed questions; when you start a mock exam and its length; when you finish one, with its length and a score band (under 50%, 50–69%, 70–84% or 85% and up), never the exact score; when you switch the question language; when you tap “Report an error”; when the purchase screen is shown; when a purchase is started or completed; and restores. The events never contain your answers, which questions you got right or wrong, or anything you type. They include the app version, device model, iOS version and language, and are linked to a random identifier created by the app, not to your name, email or Apple ID. PostHog stores these events in the United States. It uses your IP address only to estimate an approximate location (country and city), then discards it; we configure it not to keep IP addresses. We use this data only to improve the app.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple; we never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to check whether the unlock has been bought. RevenueCat receives your App Store transaction information and an anonymous app user identifier.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t ask for your name, email or any account.
  • We don’t show ads or use advertising identifiers.
  • We don’t track you across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • We don’t sell or share your data, except with the service providers named above for the purposes described.

Retention and your choices

Deleting the app removes your answers and progress from your iPhone. The copy in your iCloud account is not removed, so your progress comes back if you reinstall the app. To erase your answers and progress on this iPhone and in iCloud, use Settings › Delete my progress in the app (your settings, such as languages and exam date, stay). Usage statistics are kept for up to one year. Because they are anonymous we usually cannot link them to you, but you can email us with any request about your data and we will help as far as we can. Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to answer them and fix the reported question.

Children

Licencia Lista is a study tool for adults preparing for a license exam and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
